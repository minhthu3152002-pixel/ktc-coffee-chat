Small pill label for tags, tech stacks, and statuses.

```jsx
<Badge tone="blue">C/C++</Badge>
<Badge tone="green">5 vị trí</Badge>
<Badge tone="pink">Hot</Badge>
```

Tones: `blue` (default) · `pink` · `cyan` · `green` · `amber` · `gray`. Keep rows short — 3–4 badges max under a heading or inside a card.
